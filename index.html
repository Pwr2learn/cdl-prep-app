Help & Support

A quick manual on how to use the CDL Pro-Prep Portal.

What is this app for?

This application is a focused study tool designed to help you prepare for the New York State CDL-10 Class B and Bus exams. It contains over 400 unique practice questions covering General Knowledge, Passenger Transport, Air Brakes, and Pre-Trip inspections. The questions are available in both English and official DMV-accurate Spanish.

Content Accuracy (CDL-10 Grounding)

All questions in this application are strictly grounded in the official New York State CDL-10 Commercial Driver’s Manual. No active question remains in the app unless it is mapped to one of the supported test banks (General Knowledge, Passenger Transport, or Air Brakes). School bus-specific material is deliberately excluded.

How Practice Modes Work

  • Individual Tests: Focus on one specific area (e.g., Air Brakes) at a time.
  • Full Simulator: A comprehensive test that simulates the real exam, pulling questions from General Knowledge, Passenger, and Air Brakes. You must pass each section to pass the simulator.
  • Weak Areas Review: Generates a custom test containing only questions from categories where your average score is below the required passing threshold.

The Question Pool

To ensure you study effectively, this app tracks which questions you have already answered. When you start a new practice test, it will prioritize unseen questions. This prevents you from memorizing the same repeated questions and forces you to learn the entire manual.

Tip: If you run out of unseen questions in a category, the app will prompt you to reset the pool. You can also manually reset your question pool at the bottom of the Practice Modes page.

Changing Languages & Difficulty

  • Language: Use the language selector in the sidebar (or mobile menu) to switch between English and Spanish. This changes the language of the test questions instantly. The main navigation remains in English.
  • Difficulty: On the Practice Modes page, use the difficulty selector to change the required passing score (Normal 80%, Hard 95%, Difficult 98%). This affects how your Weak Areas are calculated.
